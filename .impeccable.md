# Capture Lab — Homepage Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

IT and operations leaders at mid-market/enterprise companies — CIOs, Heads of IT, IT Service Management (ITSM) leads, support/ops directors, and the technical evaluators who report to them. They research during the workday, are skeptical of AI hype, and are scarred by long, failed automation projects. They care most about: setup cost/time, security & data control, transparency (no black boxes), and whether it works _on top of the stack they already run_ (ServiceNow, Jira, Zendesk, Salesforce, Slack, Outlook, Confluence).

### Jobs to be done

- Automate repetitive service/ops processes without a multi-month consulting engagement.
- Make AI agents actually reliable by grounding them in how _their_ org works.
- Prove to security/compliance that an AI automation is safe, auditable, and controllable.
- Get visibility into processes that currently live in tickets, logs, docs, and people's heads.

### Brand Personality

**Precise · institutional · quietly confident.** A serious enterprise-grade research lab, not a flashy consumer AI startup. Editorial restraint over hype. Emotional goals: confidence, trust, "this is safe and serious," competence. The site should lower the fear that AI automation is a black box or a rip-and-replace project.

### Aesthetic Direction (locked: "Evolve current identity")

- **Theme: light.** Warm ivory base (`#fbf9f7`) — derived from a daytime B2B research context and brand continuity. Deep near-black panels (`#181113`) punctuate for contrast (hero visual frame, integrations, transparency/security, final CTA).
- **Type:** Libre Caslon Condensed (display serif, editorial — kept); Inter (body — _intentionally kept_ for cohesion with the product app, documented exception to the Inter ban); JetBrains Mono (micro-labels/eyebrows only, never body).
- **Color:** Capture Lab pink `#a53860` as a rare accent (~10% weight) — pill-CTA dot, key numerals, link underlines, active tab marker, small emphasis. Neutrals tinted warm. New tints authored in OKLCH.
- **Layout:** generous whitespace (edra-inspired), asymmetric editorial 12-col grids, left-aligned, hairline section rules. Vary layout per section — no card-soup, no identical card grids.
- **Motion (Framer Motion):** one orchestrated hero load with staggered reveals; `whileInView` section reveals (reduced-motion aware); micro-interactions on buttons/tabs/cards; a signature interactive **tabbed "How it works"** (Discover → Build → Run) with animated panel swaps; an animated integrations marquee. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Anti-references:** purple→blue gradients, neon-on-dark, glassmorphism, gradient text, identical icon-topped cards, the big-number hero-metric template. None of these.

### Positioning (locked: "Full reposition")

Capture Lab = an **agentic automation platform**. It learns how your business operates from the systems you already use, turns that into executable + transparent knowledge, and lets AI agents run those processes reliably. Drop all CRE-prospecting copy.

### Value props to feature

1. **Self-building automations** — builds the automations itself from your data; you don't script them.
2. **Minimal / zero setup** — no interviews, workshops, or months of config. Point it at your systems; it learns.
3. **Learns institutional knowledge** — reverse-engineers how your org actually runs from tickets, logs, docs, conversations.
4. **Security** — your data stays in your tenant, scoped/least-privilege access, full audit trail, not used for training. (Frame as architecture, not unverified certifications.)
5. **Integrations with IT tools** — ServiceNow, Jira, Zendesk, Salesforce, Slack, Outlook, Confluence.
6. **Full transparency** — every agent action is grounded, auditable, reviewable, human-approvable. No black box.

### Honesty constraints

- Do **not** fabricate specific customer names, logos, or testimonials (edra's HubSpot/ASOS are theirs, not ours).
- Do **not** assert specific compliance certifications as fact; describe concrete security architecture instead.
- Keep the real backers (CMU, Harvard, Pear VC).
- Product visuals are built in code (no CRE screenshots — they no longer match the positioning).

### Design Principles

1. **Editorial, not advertorial.** Serif headlines, hairlines, whitespace, restraint. Earn trust through craft.
2. **Accent is rare.** Pink appears where it means something; everywhere else is warm neutral.
3. **Show the mechanism.** Convey "learns → builds → runs" with real interactive/animated structure, not stock imagery.
4. **Motion serves comprehension.** Orchestrate the hero; reveal on scroll; never animate just to animate. Always reduced-motion safe.
5. **Vary the rhythm.** Each section gets a distinct layout and spacing signature; light and dark alternate with intent.
